01 / INTERNSHIP
Content opportunity scoring
30,000 records
Python · pandas · scikit-learn

Finding the next content refresh opportunity

Investigating declining pages and comparing rules with machine learning to prioritise action.

Explore the case study

The question

Which pages should be reviewed first when the team has limited capacity?

My approach

Prepared 30,000 content-performance records, explored search volume, impressions, content length and click-through rate by position. Trained logistic regression, decision trees and random forests.

What I learned

On held-out clients, a depth-2 tree reached Precision@20 of 0.60 versus 0.45 for the hand rule. At 50 selections, the hand rule was stronger: 0.62 versus 0.56. The best method depends on review capacity.

I also identified target leakage when a trend feature produced perfect Precision@50. High scores need scrutiny before model selection.

Implementation

Combined an autoencoder, safety routing and Random Forest classification. Added SHAP and LIME explanations and a Streamlit inference application.

Held-out results

Attack recall: 99.79%. False-negative rate: 0.21%. Evil Twin F1 score: 97.39%.

Scope of the evidence

These metrics describe the AWID3 held-out evaluation. They do not establish performance on live enterprise traffic.
